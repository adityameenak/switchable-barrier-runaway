## Caveats

**Silica aerogel blanket**
- Industrial pipe insulation grade; battery-specific aerogel sheets are thinner (~1-3 mm) and may differ in k and density.
- k measured at 2 psi; cell swelling compresses barriers further.

**Phlogopite mica paper laminate**
- Through-plane k given at 50 C only; datasheet's 0.21 W/(m K) at 250 C is in-plane, so the high-temperature through-plane value is unknown.

**Alumina-silica ceramic fibre**
- Blanket data, not the thin papers used between cells.
- No k below 260 C, so normal-operation k must be extrapolated.

**Conductive silicone gap pad**
- Rated only to 200 C: the silicone degrades well below cell runaway temperatures, so its high-temperature behaviour is not modelled by a constant k.
- Values not yet checked against the Henkel TDS.

**Paraffin / expanded-graphite composite PCM**
- Developed for spacecraft electronics, not battery barriers.
- The 9.99 W/(m K) measurement temperature is not given in the text; 25 C is assumed.
- Paper reports k decreasing with temperature but the k(T) curve is only in a figure.
- Pressure-aligned EG: the reported k may be the in-plane value, not through-plane.
- Paraffin is flammable and boils/decomposes far below runaway temperatures; behaviour above ~85 C is unmeasured.

**Graphene / expandable-microsphere thermal switch**
- Switch ratio ~13x, far below the idealised 100x in params.py.
- Switches 'within 30 s'; the model currently assumes instant switching.
- Expanding microspheres suggest a one-way (irreversible) switch; reversibility is not stated in the abstract.
- Only the abstract was available: density, cp and thickness are unknown.
